To You, ❤️

My Heart Speaks...

Before this,
we were just classmates 🙂
Everything felt normal.

But one moment changed something.
It played like a movie in my mind 🎞️
Again and again.

It was not just a simple day,
not a random second.
Something quietly changed inside me.

The sun touched your curly hair,
and it shined so softly ✨
That moment felt special,
and I couldn't take my eyes off you.

I didn't know why then,
but my mind paused,
and my heart noticed 💭❤️

I wanted to know you more.
Not just as a classmate,
but as someone special.

I found an excuse to talk to you.
On Voters' Day, I spoke to you for the first time 🗳️
I was nervous,
but you were so friendly 🙂

From that moment, we started talking.
We still talk,
and I hope we always will 🤍

There are many things I want to say,
but sometimes I stay silent.
I'm afraid it might change everything.

You know what this is.
We faced ups and downs.
You were there for me —
not as a lover,
but as a true friend 🤝

That's when I realized,
you are someone who truly cares.

I respect your feelings 🌱
Even when love was not your choice.

One day, I gave you a rose plant 🌹
not as a romantic gesture,

A rose plant in my hands,
not as a symbol of love,
but as a symbol of care.

I hoped it would grow and bloom 🌿
I felt so happy when you smiled
and said it was cute 😊

I thought that was the end,
but it wasn't.
It was just the beginning.

One moment.
One look.
One second.
It changed everything ✨

Your rose plant is growing well 🌹
A reminder of that moment,
and the feelings that learned to grow.

Maybe this isn't a love story yet,
but it's where my feelings
started to bloom 🌸

On this Valentine's Day,
I want to say...
You mean the world to me 💖

— Dinesh 🤍💫
